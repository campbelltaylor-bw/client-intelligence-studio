## The one-line frame

**BridgeWise is investment AI for financial institutions.** Embedded into financial platforms, our regulated investment AI powers a premium guided investment experience for every type of investor, 24/7. **The institution is always the customer. The end investor is who the institution serves.**

Coverage is **60,000 global assets**, powering **100+ of the world's leading financial institutions** reaching **35M+ end users**, with offices in the US, Latin America, Europe, East Asia and the Middle East.

## What we sell

A core embedded investment AI, not a menu of per-asset-class modules. Institutions buy one system and configure it to their platform, their asset classes and their regulatory environment.

Two technical pillars: a **machine learning engine** trained on 20+ years of historical data analysing 280+ fundamental parameters per security, and a **proprietary Micro Language Model** that generates human-readable output in the reader's preferred language. Domain-specific and auditable, built for regulated finance — not a general-purpose LLM.

### The four capabilities

**Actionable Intelligence.** Proprietary analysis across 60,000 global assets, refreshed daily, turning market data into a clear reason to act with every angle the recommendation rests on: rating, fundamentals, technicals, analyst consensus, peer comparison, social sentiment, news, and the story behind the move. Nothing is asserted without the evidence sitting beside it. This is the ground the other three stand on.

**Always-on Awareness.** Round-the-clock monitoring across every holding and every watchlist on the platform. Each market move is checked against who actually holds it, who follows it, and whether it changes anything for that person, before anything is sent. The investor receives three things together: the moment itself, the reasoning behind it, and the statistical insight on the probability and potential return of acting. This is a filter rather than a firehose, and the filter is the product.

**Conversational Concierge.** **Bridget™**, the world's first compliant AI chat for investing, answers whatever an investor asks next, in their own language, grounded in verified financial data. It runs on structured financial intelligence prepared in advance through an Intent-Driven Execution Engine rather than on retrieval at query time, so the numbers in an answer are deterministic and only the language is generated. It explains and educates. It never places the trade.

**Agentic Investing.** **pAI** monitors, analyses and proposes moves, and the human approves before anything happens. In retail, this gives every investor a personal wealth manager they never had access to. In advisory, it does the advisor's homework and hands them a ready-to-approve move. The approval step is not a detail, it is the product.

### The Ready Moment — our core sales vocabulary

Three of the four capabilities build every **Ready Moment**. An investor acts only when three things are true at once, and a platform that delivers two of them still gets nothing.

- **A reason to act.** Always-on Awareness finds the moment that matters to this specific person.
- **The confidence to act.** Actionable Intelligence makes the case, with the evidence beside it.
- **A path to act.** Bridget answers the last question and carries them to the trade.

Every Ready Moment makes the next one smarter. Every action sharpens personalisation, and sharper personalisation powers the next moment. Across thousands of moments this stops being a feature and becomes a relationship.

Agentic Investing sits in the **Frontier** tier — a separate, higher-priced offering, not an upgrade path.

## Where to focus

Highest priority, in order:

1. **Online trading platforms and CFD brokerages** — proven product-market fit, most case studies, and the only segment where the full proof chain applies.
2. **Stock exchanges** — strategic, limited competition, strong reference customers.
3. **Banks with investment services** — largest deals, strongest retention, and the only segment that buys both offerings.
4. **Buy-side quants and systematic funds** — fastest-growing, unlocked by the Context Analytics acquisition, reachable through the alternative-data conference circuit.

Neobanks and robo-advisors are fast-growing opportunities where speed-to-market via API is the primary buying trigger.

Entry point in most organisations: **CPO/VP of Product or Head of Trading** as champion, **CEO** as economic buyer at mid-size firms, **CIO or Head of Digital** at larger institutions. Every deal requires proactive engagement with **Compliance** to prevent late-stage blocks.

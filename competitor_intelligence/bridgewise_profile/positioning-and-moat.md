## Head-to-head

| Against | The argument |
| --- | --- |
| **TipRanks** — aggregates human analyst ratings | BridgeWise generates proprietary analysis covering the assets human analysts never touch |
| **Bloomberg / Refinitiv** | Broader coverage at a fraction of the cost, with white-label flexibility these incumbents do not provide |
| **Building in-house** | Time-to-market, and the determinism of the architecture — a RAG-based internal build is probabilistic, and probabilistic is not tolerable in regulated finance |

## The four deepest moats

**Stock exchange partnerships.** No competitor holds relationships with JPX, B3, SIX, TASE and LSE simultaneously.

**The regulated position** in the markets where BridgeWise operates.

**Multilingual capability** that most competitors cannot match in non-English markets.

**The four capabilities working as one system** — taking an institution from analysis, through proactive engagement and conversation, to agentic action inside one embedded product.

The **Context Analytics acquisition** ($13M, February 2026) adds proprietary alternative data processing, creating an end-to-end pipeline from raw signal to client-facing insight.

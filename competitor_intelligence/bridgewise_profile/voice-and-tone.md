How BridgeWise sounds, and how we want the reader to feel. This file governs the *feel*. The mechanical rules, the word lists and the pre-ship pass live in [`writing-rules.md`](writing-rules.md).

Read this before drafting anything external: web, email, social, PR, sales copy, event material.

## How we want our audience to feel

Five emotional drivers. Good copy hits at least one on purpose.

1. **FOMO.** A desire to actively adopt innovation and take part in the technological transformation already underway.
2. **Urgency.** A motivation to start moving and seize opportunities that move the needle, now.
3. **Eureka!** A sense of revelation and relief: finally, a solution that fits us like a glove.
4. **Confidence.** Reassurance and safety: we're in good hands and good company. This is not a bet. This brand carries domain authority, backed by proven best practices.
5. **Class.** A sense of distinction: this feels premium, polished, elevated and sophisticated.

## The three voice pillars

From the brand strategy. Each one is shown as the flat version most drafts land on, and the version we want.

### We're ambitious

We share our customers' drive to seize opportunities that drive growth and break new ceilings.

| Flat | Ours |
| --- | --- |
| "Help every investor make informed investment decisions." | "Put a wealth of intelligence to work for every investor." |
| "Tap into new opportunities for growth." | "Keep investors magnetized with a stellar investment experience." |

### We're grounded

We trade abstraction for specifics, packaging bold ideas in tangible terms.

| Flat | Ours |
| --- | --- |
| "Trusted by leading financial institutions worldwide." | "Over 100 global financial institutions trust Bridgewise to reshape their investment experience." |
| "Create new opportunities for growth." | "Deepen customer relationships and grow LTV and share of wallet." |

### We're crisp

We are incisive, cutting through complexity with direct, precise language.

| Flat | Ours |
| --- | --- |
| "Bridgewise combines diligent compliance with advanced AI tech." | "Where vertical depth meets AI-native scale." |
| "Enable investors to act on market insights." | "Investment guidance, from insight to action." |

## Tone flex

Same voice, different volume.

- Launches lean **ambitious**.
- Compliance and product copy lean **grounded and crisp**.
- Social can carry more energy, but never hype.
- Limit exclamation points. No sensationalism, anywhere.
